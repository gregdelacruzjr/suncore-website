What we offer

Three ways to power your property

Every property's energy needs are different. Suncore designs, sizes, and installs the setup that fits yours — whether that means full independence from the grid, seamless backup power, or maximizing savings on your existing connection.

🔋

Off-grid

Fully independent from the utility grid. Solar panels charge a battery bank that powers your property directly — ideal for remote sites or anywhere grid power is unreliable or unavailable.

🔌

On-grid

Connected directly to the utility grid — solar power offsets your usage, and excess energy can be sent back for credit. The most affordable setup, best where grid power is stable.

🔀

Hybrid

The best of both — solar and battery power your home first, with the grid as backup. You save on bills day to day and stay powered during outages.
