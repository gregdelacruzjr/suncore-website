## Financing

**Q: Do you offer financing or payment plans for solar installations?**
A: Yes, Suncore offers flexible payment options including full upfront payment and installment plans through partner financing institutions. Contact us for current financing terms and eligibility requirements.

**Q: What's the typical payback period for a solar PV system in the Philippines?**
A: Most residential systems in the Philippines pay for themselves within 4-7 years, depending on system size, electricity consumption, and local sun hours. Your personalized proposal will include a specific payback estimate based on your usage.

**Q: Are there government incentives or net metering benefits I should know about?**
A: The Philippines' Renewable Energy Act provides incentives for solar adopters, including net metering programs that let you sell excess energy back to the grid. We'll walk you through what applies to your specific setup during your consultation.

## System Sizing

**Q: How do you determine what size solar system I need?**
A: We calculate your ideal system size based on your average monthly electricity consumption, available roof space, sun exposure in your area, and your energy goals (partial offset vs. full independence).

**Q: What information do you need from me to create a proposal?**
A: We'll need a recent electricity bill (to understand your consumption), your property address (for sun hour data), and basic details about your roof (size, orientation, shading).

**Q: How much roof space is required for a typical residential system?**
A: A typical 3kWp system requires around 15-20 square meters of usable roof space, though this varies by panel wattage and roof layout. Your proposal will include exact specifications for your system.

## Warranty

**Q: What warranty comes with the solar panels and inverter?**
A: Solar panels typically come with a 25-year performance warranty and 10-12 year product warranty. Inverters generally carry a 5-10 year warranty. Exact terms depend on the manufacturer and will be specified in your proposal.

**Q: What happens if a component fails after installation?**
A: Components under warranty are repaired or replaced according to manufacturer terms. Suncore also provides post-installation support to help coordinate any warranty claims on your behalf.

## Installation Process

**Q: How long does installation typically take?**
A: Most residential installations take 1-3 days, depending on system size and roof complexity, after permits and equipment are secured.

**Q: Do I need any permits before installation begins?**
A: Yes, solar installations typically require a building permit and, for grid-tied systems, approval from your local electric utility. Suncore assists with the permitting process.

**Q: Will my power be interrupted during installation?**
A: Brief interruptions may occur during final connection to your electrical panel, but these are typically minimal and scheduled to minimize disruption.

## Net Metering

**Q: What is net metering and how does it work?**
A: Net metering allows you to export excess solar energy back to the grid in exchange for credits on your electric bill, effectively using the grid as a "battery" for surplus energy.

**Q: Do I need to apply for net metering separately, or does Suncore handle it?**
A: Suncore assists with the net metering application process with your local utility, though final approval and timelines depend on your utility provider.
